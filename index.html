MERA stock agent showdown

Two agents. One desk. Your call.

Each round, two trading agents pitch a move on the same volatile market. You approve or reject each pitch before the clock runs out. Ten rounds, real consequences, no real money.

Agent ARIA
momentum desk
Chases breakouts and volume spikes. Confident, fast, occasionally chasing ghosts.
Agent VESPER
contrarian desk
Hunts oversold names and mean reversion. Patient, skeptical, sometimes early.
  • 12 seconds per round to approve or reject each pitch — no decision reads as a reject.
  • Approve a winner, earn points. Approve a loser, lose points.
  • Reject a loser, small reward. Reject a winner, small penalty for the miss.
  • Higher agent confidence means bigger swings, up or down.

"Play the desk" — you approve or reject each pitch. "Watch ARIA vs VESPER" — no buttons, the two agents each back their own call and whoever reads the market right takes the round.
